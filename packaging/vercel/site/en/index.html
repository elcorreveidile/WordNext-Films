Your video, edited in the browser, with no account.

WordNext Films is an open-source non-linear video editor. Arrange your clips on a timeline, cut, adjust audio and export. It all runs in your browser: your files are never uploaded to a server.

Open WordNext Films View the source

Free and open source (MIT or Apache-2.0). Works in browsers with WebGPU or WebGL2; a desktop computer is recommended.

The first visit downloads the app; after that your browser keeps it and it opens faster.

What you can do

The tools of a video editor, on a single screen.

Timeline

Arrange video, audio and image clips on tracks, trim them and move them precisely.

Precise editing

Time is computed exactly, with no rounding drift, so cuts land where you want them.

Audio

Mix the tracks and hear the result while you work.

Export

Render the final video and download it to your computer.

Recovery

Your project is saved automatically in your browser and reopens if you close the tab by mistake.

Your language

The interface follows your system language, and you can change it from the menu.

Getting started

Three steps and you are editing.

  1. Open the appPress “Open WordNext Films”. It loads in the tab, nothing to install.
  2. Bring your clipsDrag them onto the page or pick them from your computer.
  3. Edit and exportWork on the timeline and download the result.

Your files stay yours

No server processes your video: the application runs in your browser.

  • No account or sign-up.
  • Your files are not uploaded.
  • Open source: you can check what it does.

Where it comes from

WordNext Films is WordNext's build of FilmCraft, an open-source video editor written in Rust, offered in the browser and in Spanish. The code of this build is on GitHub. It keeps the licences and authorship of the original project.
